AgSaver Privacy Policy

Effective date: 10 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Glenn Bonnet ("I", "me", "my") handles information when you use AgSaver (the "App"). It applies only to AgSaver.

Your privacy matters. I collect as little information as possible, and I never sell your personal information.

1. Information you enter in the App

AgSaver lets you record information for your own use, such as agricultural equipment details, service records, parts, expenses, work sessions and hour logs, fuel, field boundaries, crop, yield, spray and input records, photos and notes.

This information is stored on your device and, if you are signed in to iCloud, synced through your own private iCloud database, which only you can access. Optional crew sharing is the one exception, described below. I do not have access to the content you enter. Backups, sync files, spreadsheets and reports are created only when you ask, and go wherever you choose to save or share them.

2. Permissions

AgSaver may ask for access to your camera, photo library or notifications, for example to add a photo of a machine or receipt, or to alert you when maintenance is due. This access is only used for the feature you choose.

Location: AgSaver uses your location while a work session is running, to record the path your machine travels so you can see where you worked on a map, which fields you worked in and how many acres you covered. You can also record a field boundary by driving around it. Routes and field boundaries are saved with your own data and are not sent to me.

Weather: to show the weather and spray window, and to fill in conditions for work sessions and spray records, AgSaver asks Apple Weather for the forecast at your current location, or at a saved field or work path. These requests are handled by Apple under its privacy policy and are not sent to me.

Crew sharing: if you start or join a crew, then while a work session is running AgSaver shares the name you choose, your machine's position and recent work path, the machine and implement names, and whether you are working or paused. This is stored in AgSaver's iCloud public database under your crew code, and only people who have the code can look it up. Positions are not sent when no work session is running, and leaving the crew removes your entry. Crew sharing is off unless you turn it on.

You can change any permission at any time in your device settings.

3. Information collected automatically

The App may collect limited, non-identifying technical information, such as crash reports and basic usage statistics, to help fix bugs and improve it. This information is not used to identify you personally.

4. Purchases and subscriptions

If you make an in-app purchase or subscribe, payment is handled entirely by the Apple App Store. I never receive or store your payment card details.

5. How information is used

Any information I do receive is used only to:

  • provide, maintain and improve the App;
  • fix bugs and resolve technical problems;
  • reply to you when you contact me for support.

6. Sharing of information

I do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. Information is only shared with service providers needed to run the App (such as the App Store), or where required by law.

7. When you contact me

If you email me, I will receive your email address and anything you include in your message. I use this only to respond to you, and I do not add you to any mailing list without your permission.

8. Data retention and deletion

Because your data is stored on your device, you can delete it at any time within the App or by uninstalling the App. Data stored in your own iCloud can be managed through your device or iCloud settings. If you have emailed me and would like that correspondence deleted, just ask.

9. Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information held about you, or to object to how it is used. To make a request, contact me using the details below.

11. Changes to this policy

I may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

12. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please email info@glennbonnet.com.
